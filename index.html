PDF Merge & Split

Combine several PDFs into one, or split a PDF into pages — all on your device.

Do not paste passwords, one-time codes, card or bank numbers, or ID numbers.

Merge PDFs

Add two or more PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, then merge them into a single PDF.

Drag and drop PDF files here, or click to browse.

You can add as many PDFs as you like.

    Split a PDF

    Choose one PDF, then enter page ranges to extract, or split every page into its own file.

      Client-side PDF processing may not support every PDF feature. Complex layouts, encrypted PDFs, or broken files may fail. The tool is not a substitute for a full desktop PDF editor.
